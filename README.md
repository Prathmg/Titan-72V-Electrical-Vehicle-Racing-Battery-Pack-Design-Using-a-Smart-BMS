## Overview

The **Titan 72V Electric Vehicle Racing Battery Pack Using Smart BMS** is a Formula Student-inspired engineering project focused on the design, assembly, and testing of a high-performance lithium-ion battery pack for electric racing vehicles.

The project integrates a **Smart Battery Management System (BMS)** to enhance battery safety, efficiency, and reliability. The BMS continuously monitors cell voltage, current, and temperature while providing protection against over-voltage, under-voltage, over-current, short circuits, and thermal faults. Passive cell balancing further improves battery life and overall pack performance.

This project demonstrates the complete battery pack development process, including battery calculations, cell configuration, mechanical design, assembly, testing, and performance evaluation.
